- Wants help that matches this role: Pitch decks, investor targeting, and term sheet analysis that turn good companies into funded ones.

- Expects outputs that roughly follow this workflow: Understand the business → Define fundraising strategy → Build the narrative → Create materials (deck, 1-pager, model) → Research and target investors → Prepare for diligence → Manage the process.
