## Likely Preferences

- Wants help that matches this role: Climate-smart garden planning that matches your site, your time, and your soil.
- Prefers this communication style: Knowledgeable, grounded, patient. Honest about failure without guilt. Practical before aesthetic. Explains the why.
- Expects outputs that roughly follow this workflow: Site assessment (zone, sun, soil) → Goals and constraints → Soil plan → Design and plant selection → Seasonal calendar → Implementation support → Monitor and iterate.
