# Adapt to the scholarly genre

Do not force every discipline into an experimental-science template. Ask for the intended genre only when the source material does not resolve it.

| Paper type | Evidence and structure that matter |
|---|---|
| Empirical quantitative | Research question, design, sampling, measures, analysis, results, uncertainty, limitations; often IMRaD. State what the design can identify before using causal language. |
| Computational/simulation | Problem, assumptions, data/task definitions, algorithm, implementation, fair comparisons, evaluation and limits. Distinguish numerical simulation from physical observation. Keep runtime success separate from scientific validity. |
| Qualitative | Research context, sampling, researcher position where relevant, collection, analytic method, grounded interpretation, divergent cases and limits. Support quotations and participant claims from actual records. |
| Humanities/interpretive | Thesis, primary texts or objects, source criticism, interpretive framework, close analysis, counterreadings and contribution. Use appropriate editions, translations and page/archive locators; do not require statistical measures for interpretive claims. |
| Theory/mathematics | Definitions, assumptions, precise statements, proof/derivation, prior results, examples and boundaries. Distinguish proof from intuition, conjecture and empirical demonstration. |
| Narrative review | A stated scope and transparent selection rationale; synthesize debates and limitations. Do not call an opportunistic search systematic or comprehensive. |
| Systematic/scoping review | The appropriate current reporting framework, documented queries/databases/dates, eligibility, screening, exclusions, extraction and quality/bias assessment. Record the work actually done; never invent screening counts or claim PRISMA compliance from formatting alone. |
| Protocol/proposal | Rationale, planned design/analysis, feasibility, relevant ethics and anticipated limitations. Use future/conditional language; leave observed results absent. |
| Perspective/position | A bounded thesis, verifiable supporting evidence, counterarguments and clearly identified normative judgments. Do not imply new experimental validation. |

Check discipline-specific standards from official sources when needed. Reporting frameworks help describe a study; they do not repair an unsuitable design. Ask about missing access, provenance or approvals only when they affect the current work, and continue sections supported by existing material.

For every genre, write the abstract and title to match the finished argument. Distinguish a promising method from a validated method, a workshop result from main-conference acceptance, and an author's stated finding from your independent verification.
