# Choose a craft card

Start with the source draft and its preservation contract. Choose at most two cards; no card is a mandatory feature of good prose.

| Problem | Card |
|---|---|
| Criticism that feels indiscriminately positive, negative, or certain. | [Qualify a judgment without dissolving it](01-qualified-judgment.md) |
| An essay begins with an abstract thesis but already contains an observation that motivates it. | [Let an observed detail open a question](02-detail-to-question.md) |
| An edit breaks a connected thought into equally emphatic fragments, or treats length as a defect. | [Let a long sentence accumulate meaning](03-sustained-syntax.md) |
| A cleanup removes recurring words or openings without checking their function. | [Preserve repetition that changes the pressure](04-purposeful-repetition.md) |
| Satire or a roundup sounds uniformly earnest, or every line strains to be a punchline. | [Let humor expose the narrator's position](05-comic-distance.md) |
| An interview becomes a string of generic questions or edited replies all sound alike. | [Follow an answer and keep speakers distinct](06-distinct-speakers.md) |
| A polished rewrite makes a provisional account sound settled. | [Keep an inquiry's uncertainty visible](07-visible-inquiry.md) |
| An argument relies on a source's wording, but the evidence and interpretation blur. | [Separate the evidence from the writer's inference](08-attribution-and-inference.md) |
| An ending repeats the thesis after the draft has already made its point. | [End by returning with a changed meaning](09-concrete-return.md) |
| A review asserts taste without specifying the work that prompted it. | [Explain where a response comes from](10-specific-criticism.md) |
| An advice passage treats a person as an obstacle, or adds an unsupported sympathetic story. | [Consider a perspective without inventing its interior](11-empathy-with-limits.md) |
| A response discusses a large social problem while leaving the writer's immediate question untouched. | [Replace an unhelpful abstraction with a precise distinction](12-action-and-distinction.md) |

The 24 development sources span six bylines and several forms. Twelve other articles by three separate writers were reserved outside this skill package for the first evaluation. Original source texts and held-out inputs are not distributed here.

Historical capture dates are retained separately from index publication dates. Date fields may differ by a day because of publishing-system or timezone conventions. Author names and article types were checked against the captured pages; notably, *Interview with … Jesus?* is a book review. For interviews and advice, distinguish the byline from the speakers or letter writers.

These annotations describe selected passages. They neither endorse an entire article nor establish universal style rules. Some cards deliberately discuss overreach in the source. Use the current user's material for facts and voice; preserve uncertainty and all protected text.
