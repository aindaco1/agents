# Qualify a judgment without dissolving it

Use when: Criticism that feels indiscriminately positive, negative, or certain.

Observed move: Identify the precise respect in which the judgment holds. In the Gosford Park opening, modest praise becomes a reason to value lightness. The Rendell review explains why an apparently uninviting reading experience remains compelling. Miller's myth essay questions its starting theory rather than silently promoting it to truth.

Apply it: Use the draft's actual evidence to distinguish what works, for whom, and under what conditions. A qualification should change the claim's meaning. It need not create an equal-and-opposite paragraph.

Limits: Do not add a concession for balance or weaken justified certainty. Scientific uncertainty and contractual scope are content to preserve, not rhythm to smooth.
