# Keep an inquiry's uncertainty visible

Use when: A polished rewrite makes a provisional account sound settled.

Observed move: Leonard labels explanations for the airport signs as speculation while awaiting a response. His algae column changes the apparent legislative stakes after a reporting inquiry. The route to a claim helps define what the writer actually knows.

Apply it: Preserve whether a detail is observed, reported by someone, inferred, or unresolved. A concrete next question can be more useful than an unsupported answer. If the source records a correction, retain its effect on the conclusion.

Limits: Do not manufacture calls, sources, doubt, or investigative drama. Genre permits varying amounts of process detail; a concise operational update may need only the uncertainty and next step.
