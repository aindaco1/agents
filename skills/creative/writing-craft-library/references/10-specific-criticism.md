# Explain where a response comes from

Use when: A review asserts taste without specifying the work that prompted it.

Observed move: Zacharek acknowledges that other viewers respond differently before naming the scene construction she finds distancing. Miller distinguishes Rice's earlier habits from the particular storytelling choices that work in the reviewed book.

Apply it: Attach a judgment to a supplied formal choice, scene, or reader effect. Distinguish personal response from a claim about every audience. An unfavorable review can remain precise rather than uniformly hostile.

Limits: Do not invent details about a work or diagnose its creator's mind. Respect a user who wants a different stance; reference criticism cannot supply their opinion.
