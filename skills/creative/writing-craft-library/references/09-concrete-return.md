# End by returning with a changed meaning

Use when: An ending repeats the thesis after the draft has already made its point.

Observed move: The Kate & Leopold review turns a late scene detail back onto its contrast between performances. Gosford Park closes with a physical feature that carries the social distinction. Williams' commentary revisits the disputed photograph from a different emotional position.

Apply it: Look for an existing object, action, or distinction whose meaning the piece has changed. It may make an adequate final sentence without another paragraph explaining it. Sometimes an explicit conclusion remains necessary.

Limits: Do not force a circular structure, introduce a new symbol at the end, or invent another person's feelings. A clear decision, limitation, or practical next step may be the appropriate ending.
