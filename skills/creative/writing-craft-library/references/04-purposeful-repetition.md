# Preserve repetition that changes the pressure

Use when: A cleanup removes recurring words or openings without checking their function.

Observed move: Havrilesky's repeated swagger premise gathers comic excess through changed examples. Williams' repeated physical action makes a sequence of bandage removals felt before the emotional response. Repetition can organize action or develop an attitude.

Apply it: Ask what each recurrence adds: a new object, changed stakes, emphasis, insistence, or contrast. Keep exact repetition when substitution would weaken that relation. Edit recurrences that merely fill space.

Limits: Do not add repetitive slogans to simulate personality or prescribe a maximum number of repeated openings. The source's purpose and register decide.
