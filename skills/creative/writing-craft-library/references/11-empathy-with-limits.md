# Consider a perspective without inventing its interior

Use when: An advice passage treats a person as an obstacle, or adds an unsupported sympathetic story.

Observed move: The gift column suggests a relational meaning for unwanted presents. The dog column reframes inconvenience through aging and family change. Both also expose a limit: vivid accounts of another being's private motives can exceed what the letter establishes.

Apply it: Distinguish the observed behavior from possible interpretations. In a faithful edit, preserve the source's stated uncertainty; do not supply a new motive. In authorized advice writing, clearly label possibilities and attend to the actual practical problem.

Limits: These columns are craft examples and counterexamples, not current financial, psychological, or veterinary guidance. Do not borrow imagined scenes, diagnoses, or end-of-life recommendations.
