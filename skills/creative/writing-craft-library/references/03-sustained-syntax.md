# Let a long sentence accumulate meaning

Use when: An edit breaks a connected thought into equally emphatic fragments, or treats length as a defect.

Observed move: The Sacks review accumulates stages of a childhood in a sustained parallel passage. Miller's Narnia essay carries an adult reader's conditions and divided attachment through longer structures. The successive clauses change the thought instead of restating it.

Apply it: Trace the relationship between clauses. Retain a long sentence when its parts remain clear and the accumulation matters. Split only at a real shift or point of confusion; a series of long sentences can be appropriate.

Limits: Do not enforce a target distribution of sentence lengths, insert breathless clauses, or preserve a confusing sentence merely because a reference is complex. Reader comprehension is the criterion.
