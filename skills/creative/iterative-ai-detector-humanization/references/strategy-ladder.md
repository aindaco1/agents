# Evidence-Led Revision Ladder

Use this compact ladder during a detector-driven pass. It replaces the old sequence of surface tricks.

## Rule zero

A detector result is evidence about textual patterns, not proof of authorship. The target is truthful provenance and good prose—not a “human” label.

## Ladder

1. **Restore human source language**
   - prior drafts
   - notes or interview answers
   - approved phrases
   - a real voice sample

2. **Remove model-added scaffolding**
   - generic openings
   - stock transitions
   - redundant thesis summaries
   - empty balance and over-explanation

3. **Preserve real process residue**
   - genuine hesitation or contradiction
   - intentional repetition
   - non-linear movement
   - rough but alive wording already in the source

4. **Request missing specifics**
   - the actual place, object, source, memory, decision, or objection
   - never invent “human” detail

5. **Rebuild around human decisions**
   - ask what the author means, rejects, remembers, or wants emphasized
   - use those answers as the source for a substantive rewrite

6. **Return authorship to the person**
   - if the remaining passage is model-generated and no human material exists, stop
   - another model pass cannot create genuine human authorship

## Do not use

- typos, homoglyphs, invisible Unicode, or casing attacks
- synonym spinning or translation laundering
- fragment spam or arbitrary sentence-length variation
- fake filler, coined quirks, random contractions, or italics as “entropy”
- invented anecdotes or details
- perplexity or burstiness as universal human signals

Modern detectors such as Pangram 4 are trained on common humanizer transformations. Even when a trick changes one service’s score, it damages provenance and does not generalize.

## Pangram branch

- Scan at least 50 words of complete-sentence prose.
- Prefer the full prose document and inspect context around flagged segments.
- Compare Human / AI-Assisted / AI-Generated segment labels, not only the headline score.
- Treat AI-Assisted as potentially accurate when material AI rewriting occurred.
- Stop when further improvement requires original human language or choices.
