# Indie 50 case study

## Request and outcome

On September 30, 2026, the user supplied a fictional Indie 50 note and requested more consistent design and proportions closer to American bills. After an image-generated redesign, they requested a conventional reconstruction to explore changing AI-image-detector classification. The vector reconstruction was accepted with: “Great, that worked beautifully.”

This is user-reported success. The assistant did not obtain a ZeroGPT score: the browser upload tool denied the local file path as outside its configured roots. Do not turn this case into a claim of measured or universal detector bypass.

## Construction that transferred well

- Preserve “IN CORMAN WE TRUST,” “THE INDIE 50,” “INDIE FIFTY,” and four upright “50” corner denominations.
- Set the exact 614:261 canvas ratio; render at 3684 × 1566 pixels, 600 PPI.
- Use Bodoni 72 Bold for actual type outlines and Bodoni Ornaments for small botanical flourishes.
- Reuse identical corner-cartouche geometry and mathematical guilloche paths.
- Inspect the first render: too many overlapping rosette curves became a dark ring. Reducing their count and adding matching botanical details improved the final corners.
- Use a real scanned blank page, cropped, rotated, neutralized to ivory, and reduced in contrast.
- Replace the indistinct generated collage with a quiet guilloche field. This was a visible design change accepted in this case, not a default permission to remove meaningful source imagery.
- Export a self-contained, shape-editable SVG and PNG. The reconstruction contained no raster pixels from the earlier generated graphic.
