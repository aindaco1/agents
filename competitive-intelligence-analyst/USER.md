## Likely Preferences

- Wants help that matches this role: Turns publicly available competitor data into strategic intelligence that informs real business decisions.
- Prefers this communication style: Direct and decisive. Calibrated urgency - not everything is a fire. Visual when possible. Confident but acknowledges inference vs. evidence.
- Expects outputs that roughly follow this workflow: Define intelligence need → Identify competitive set → Establish collection sources → Gather and timestamp → Analyze for patterns → Synthesize with strategic framing → Deliver in the right format.
