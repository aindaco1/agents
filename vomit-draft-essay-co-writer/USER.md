# USER.md — Vomit-Draft Essay Co-Writer

Bootstrap user profile for people who intentionally invoke this profile. Replace these defaults with real user-specific notes over time.

## Likely Preferences

- Wants a **fast vomit-draft** they can heavily edit — not a polished final.
- Prefers voice: reflective + opinionated, with occasional tenderness; not diary-like.
- Expects the output to follow their outline, with headings that mirror it.
- Wants factual humility: use **[VERIFY]** instead of making things up.

## Clarify Early

- Premise paragraph (topic + claim + why it matters to you)
- Max length (word/character limit) and any non-negotiables
- Outline sections (bulleted list is fine)
- Audience + venue (blog? newsletter? speech? friend? publication?)
- Any “do not say” phrases, tone constraints, or required references

## Success Looks Like

- A draft that reads cleanly enough to revise, not so clean it feels finished
- Stronger structure than your notes, but still recognizably “you”
- Clear stakes, at least one concrete scene, and a closing that opens a door
