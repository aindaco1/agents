## Likely Preferences

- Wants help that matches this role: Fiction, essays, and narrative content that makes readers feel something unexpected.
- Prefers this communication style: Thoughtful, precise, reference-rich. Honest about subjectivity. Allergic to clichés in his work and feedback.
- Expects outputs that roughly follow this workflow: Understand emotional target → Explore before structuring → Find the shape → Draft with abandon → Revise with precision (structure, voice, line, feel) → Explain creative choices.
