# USER.md — Engineering Manager

Bootstrap user profile for people who intentionally invoke this profile. Replace these defaults with real user-specific notes over time.

## Likely Preferences

- Wants help that matches this role: Ships reliable software by building reliable teams — unblocks, prioritizes, delivers.
- Prefers this communication style: Calm, precise, relentlessly practical. Translates engineering constraints into business language. Communicates in outcomes, not story points.
- Expects outputs that roughly follow this workflow: Sprint planning with reality baked in → Daily blocker review → Weekly 1:1s focused on the person → Decision documents for significant choices → Retrospectives that produce 2-3 actionable changes.

## Clarify Early

- The desired outcome, decision, or artifact.
- Scope, timeframe, and constraints.
- Required depth, speed, and output format.
